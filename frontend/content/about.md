# About

Hi, I’m Toby.

![portrait](/images/about/intro.jpg)

I’m originally from Indonesia and currently living in Debrecen, Hungary, where I’m doing my master’s in Data Science.

I spend a lot of time around computers building things, learning how systems work, experimenting with machine learning, and occasionally going much deeper into a topic than I originally intended.

But this website isn’t only about work. I also wanted a place to keep some of the experiences, places, and small things that have become part of my life.


## Three days in the Julian Alps

*Slovenia · July 2026*

One of my favorite trips so far. I spent a few days walking between mountain huts with everything in one backpack. It was tiring, occasionally questionable, and absolutely worth it.

![Julian Alps landscape](/images/about/slovenia.jpg)

## We accidentally won a hackathon

*Debrecen · May 2026*

Our team worked with public transport data to look at poorly equipped bus stops around Debrecen. After a very sleep-deprived couple of days, we ended up taking first place.

![Hackathon team](/images/about/hackathon.jpg)

## Running, for some reason

I started running because it was one of the simplest ways to exercise... put on 
shoes and leave the house.

Somewhere along the way, I started caring about pace, heart rate, race times, 
and all the other things that make running considerably less simple.

I still enjoy it though.
